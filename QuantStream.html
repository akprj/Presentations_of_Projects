⚠️ Topic 3: What If You Manually Close a Trade in WunderTrading?

If you close a trade manually in WunderTrading or directly on Kraken, the system handles it smoothly and will resume trading automatically. Here is the exact breakdown:

Scenario A: The Next Signal is a Reversal (Opposite)

This is what happens 95% of the time:

  1. The strategy detects an opposite signal (e.g. Short).
  2. It sends EXIT-ALL. Since you already closed the position, WunderTrading simply acknowledges the message with no error.
  3. The engine pauses 2.0 seconds.
  4. It sends ENTER-SHORT. Since your account is flat (0 open trades), WunderTrading immediately executes the Short trade!
  5. Both systems are now 100% resynchronized!

Scenario B: The Next Signal is in the Same Direction

If another signal occurs in the same direction before a reversal:

Because USE_STACKING = false, the script guards against opening duplicate entries in the same direction and will wait for the next reversal signal.

Pro-Tip to Reset Instantly: If you close manually and want the bot ready to take ANY direction immediately, press Ctrl + C in your terminal and restart the script. It resets its tracker to FLAT on startup!

Key Takeaway: WunderTrading never breaks if you manually close a position. On the very next reversal signal, it accepts the exit cleanly and enters the new trade flawlessly.

💰 Topic 4: Real Money Trading: Spot vs Futures (Kraken & Binance)

A complete institutional guide on fund requirements, short selling mechanics, and real account execution across Kraken and Binance with QuantStream and WunderTrading.

Do You Need Funds on Both Sides (Cash & Crypto)?
• In Futures (Perpetual Swap): NO! You only need one collateral currency (USD or USDT). You do NOT need to own SOL to short SOL!
• In Spot: YES! To buy you need cash (USD/USDT); to sell you must already physically hold the crypto (SOL).

1. The Core Difference: Spot vs Futures

Spot Account Mechanics (Asset Ownership)

You trade physical tokens:

  • Going Long (Buy): You spend USD/USDT to purchase and physically own SOL tokens in your exchange wallet.
  • Going Short (Sell): In standard Spot, you cannot open a true short from cash. You can only sell the SOL tokens you already hold back to cash.
  • Capital Required: You need Quote currency (USD/USDT) to buy, and Base cryptocurrency (SOL) to sell.
  • Strategy Mode: In Spot, the strategy becomes a 1-Way Long/Cash System: Buy dips → Sell to 100% cash → Wait in cash for the next buy.

Futures (Perpetual Swap) Mechanics (Contracts)

You trade derivative contracts, not physical tokens:

  • Going Long (Buy): The exchange reserves a portion of your USD/USDT as margin to back a long contract.
  • Going Short (Sell): The exchange reserves the exact same USD/USDT margin to open a short contract. Zero SOL required!
  • Capital Required: Only 1 single currency (USD on Kraken Futures, USDT on Binance Futures).
  • Strategy Mode: 100% Full 2-Way Swing Reversal. You capture profits when the market rallies (Long) AND when the market crashes (Short).

2. Real Money Execution: Kraken Accounts

Kraken Futures (Real Account) Current Setup

Target Symbol: SOL/USD:USD (Perpetual Swap)

  • Wallet Required: Kraken Futures Wallet funded with USD.
  • ENTER-LONG: Uses USD margin to open a leveraged or 1x Long contract.
  • EXIT-ALL: Closes the contract, unlocks your USD margin, and credits profit/loss in USD.
  • ENTER-SHORT: Opens a Short position using USD margin. When SOL price drops, your USD balance grows!
  • QuantStream Configuration: EXCHANGE_ID=krakenfutures, MARKET_TYPE=swap, SYMBOLS=SOL/USD:USD.

Kraken Spot (Real Account)

Target Symbol: SOL/USD (Spot Market)

  • Wallet Required: Kraken Spot Wallet funded with USD.
  • ENTER-LONG: Spends USD cash to buy physical SOL.
  • EXIT-ALL / EXIT-LONG: Sells physical SOL back to USD cash.
  • ENTER-SHORT: Without margin borrowing enabled, Spot cannot short from cash. The bot simply sits in 100% USD cash (preserving capital).
  • QuantStream Configuration: EXCHANGE_ID=kraken, MARKET_TYPE=spot, SYMBOLS=SOL/USD.

📐 Topic 7: Backend Mathematics, Calculations & Geometric Formulas

The strategy utilizes pure mathematical harmonic geometry across the last 5 confirmed pivots: x, a, b, c, d.

1. Leg Lengths & Fibonacci Ratios

XA = |x - a|
AB = |a - b|
BC = |b - c|
CD = |c - d|
xab = AB / XA = |b - a| / |x - a|
xad = AD / XA = |a - d| / |x - a|
abc = BC / AB = |b - c| / |a - b|
bcd = CD / BC = |c - d| / |b - c|
